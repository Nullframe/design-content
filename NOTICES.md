## Fallback fonts pack (`fonts-fallback-*` releases)

Unmodified upstream font files from the [Noto project](https://github.com/notofonts), [Noto CJK](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk) (Adobe and Google) and [Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji) (Google), and STIX Two Math from the [STIX Fonts Project](https://github.com/stipub/stixfonts), all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Each archive carries the OFL text with every family's copyright notice (`fonts/noto/OFL.txt`, `wasm-degraded-fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJKsc.txt`, `wasm-degraded-fonts/OFL-NotoColorEmoji.txt`, and from `fonts-fallback-2026-10-01` on `fonts/stix/OFL.txt`). Noto Sans CJK keeps Adobe's Reserved Font Name "Source" and STIX Two Math the STIX Fonts Project's "TM Math"; neither font is modified.

## Not here

Cisco's OpenH264 binary, which Voyager Design uses for MP4 on Linux, is never rehosted. It is downloaded from Cisco (`ciscobinary.openh264.org`) under Cisco's own binary licence. Google Fonts families that Voyager Design installs one at a time come straight from [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts).
